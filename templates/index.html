Core WSD Processing Pipeline

Step 1
Transcript & Target Input

Accepts educational lecture text and identifies the ambiguous target vocabulary item.

Step 2
Context Extraction

Extracts a configurable surrounding window (default ±10 words) around target occurrences.

Step 3
Text Normalization

Lowercasing, tokenization, stop-word filtering, POS tagging, and NLTK lemmatization.

Step 4
WordNet Synset Retrieval

Fetches all candidate senses, definitions (glosses), examples, hypernyms, and hyponyms.

Step 5
Thesaurus Expansion

Expands context vocabulary with WordNet synonyms to handle semantic paraphrase matching.

Step 6
Multi-Factor Scoring & Ranking

Computes weighted direct, lemma, example, synonym, and hypernym overlap scores to rank candidate senses.

No External AI APIs

Built purely using fundamental NLP concepts, NLTK WordNet, and transparent scoring formulas without black-box LLM dependencies.

Explainable AI (XAI)

Displays matched evidence tokens, scoring breakdowns, synonym expansions, and relative confidence for complete evaluator clarity.

Empirical Evaluation

Includes a 25-sample educational benchmark dataset evaluated against NLTK Simplified Lesk baseline with interactive visualizations.
