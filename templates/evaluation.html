Qualitative Failure & Error Analysis

The dictionary/thesaurus WSD method relies on textual context gloss overlap. Cases where custom predictions diverged from gold synsets occur primarily due to:

Sparse Gloss Vocabulary

WordNet definitions can be very concise (3–6 words). If context keywords do not explicitly appear in the gloss, overlap defaults to 0.

Fine-Grained WordNet Senses

WordNet contains highly granular distinction between related noun senses (e.g. tree as woody plant vs tree as hierarchical data structure).

Context Window Boundary

Short sentences with limited surrounding words may lack domain-specific tokens needed to tip the overlap score.
